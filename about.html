How it started

Right after my BSc in Microbiology, I packed my bags and moved to a new country where I knew no one. I went on to study Innovation and Entrepreneurship at ESEI — and I had such a great time in Barcelona, studying and working as a student.

The hands-on learning approach led me to love products and turning ideas into user-centric products, which led me to build my first-ever product mockup called Afrisphere, where we visualized the product UI and the problem it was solving.

We figured that Africans in Barcelona didn't have a community and a platform where they could meet other Africans, locate African stores, restaurants, salons, and social gatherings. It was a problem, and Afrisphere was meant to solve it.
